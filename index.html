Soal Evaluasi Bahasa Inggris: Recount Text

Materi: Recount Text (Definition, Structure, Language Features, Comprehension) | Kelas 9 SMP

Soal 1
What is the main social function of a Recount Text?
Kunci: A. Recount text bertujuan menceritakan kembali kejadian masa lalu.
Soal 2
What is the generic structure of a Recount Text in correct order?
Kunci: B. Struktur Recount Text adalah Orientation, Events, Reorientation.
Soal 3
Which part of a Recount Text introduces the characters, time, and location of the story?
Kunci: C. Orientation mengenalkan Who, Where, dan When.
Soal 4
What does the "Reorientation" paragraph contain?
Kunci: A. Reorientation berisi kesimpulan atau penutup opini penulis.
Soal 5
Which verb tense is predominantly used in writing Recount Texts?
Kunci: B. Recount text menggunakan Simple Past Tense.
Soal 6
Which group of words represents time connectives commonly used in Recount Texts?
Kunci: D. Time connectives penanda urutan waktu: First, then, after that, dst.
Soal 7
Which of the following contains ONLY past tense action verbs?
Kunci: C. Visited, went, bought, watched merupakan Verb 2 (Past Tense).
Soal 8
What is the key difference between a Recount Text and a Narrative Text?
Kunci: A. Recount tidak memiliki konflik/komplikasi seperti pada Narrative.
Soal 9
Which of the following are three common types of Recount Text?
Kunci: B. Jenis recount: Personal, Historical, dan Biographical.
Soal 10
Personal recount texts usually use which point of view?
Kunci: D. Personal recount menggunakan kata ganti orang pertama (I, We).
Soal 11
Yesterday, my family and I _____ to Kuta Beach in Bali.
Kunci: B. Past tense dari 'go' adalah 'went'.
Soal 12
She _____ not join the study tour last week because she was ill.
Kunci: A. Kalimat negatif past tense menggunakan auxiliary verb 'did'.
Soal 13
_____ you visit your grandparents during the last holiday?
Kunci: C. Kalimat tanya past tense dimulai dengan kata bantu 'Did'.
Soal 14
What is the Past Tense (Verb 2) form of "buy"?
Kunci: D. Verb 2 dari buy adalah bought.
Soal 15
What is the Past Tense (Verb 2) form of the regular verb "play"?
Kunci: B. Regular verb 'play' menjadi 'played'.
Soal 16
First, we arrived at the hotel. _____, we unpacked our bags and went to the swimming pool.
Kunci: A. 'Then' digunakan untuk menyatakan urutan peristiwa setelah 'First'.
Soal 17
Which of the following phrases is an adverb of time suitable for Recount Text?
Kunci: B. 'Two days ago' menunjuk waktu di masa lalu.
Soal 18
Choose the correct past sentence structure:
Kunci: C. 'watched' (Verb 2) + keterangan waktu 'last night'.
Soal 19
Indonesian Independence _____ on August 17th, 1945.
Kunci: A. Kalimat pasif past tense (was + V3).
Soal 20
Which of the following words is an action verb in the past form?
Kunci: D. 'Walked' adalah kata kerja aksi bentuk lampau.

Last month, my school organized a field trip to Yogyakarta. We departed from Jakarta by bus on Friday night. The journey took about ten hours, so we arrived on Saturday morning.

Our first destination was Borobudur Temple. The view from the top was breathtaking and we took many pictures together. After lunch, we visited Malioboro Street to buy some souvenirs and traditional snacks like Bakpia. Finally, in the evening, we went to the hotel to rest.

Although we were exhausted, everyone felt happy. It was a memorable experience that I will never forget.

Soal 21
What is the main topic of the text?
Kunci: A. Teks menceritakan karya wisata sekolah ke Yogyakarta.
Soal 22
How did the writer and their school group travel to Yogyakarta?
Kunci: C. Beralamat pada kalimat: "We departed from Jakarta by bus..."
Soal 23
What did the students do at Malioboro Street?
Kunci: B. "...visited Malioboro Street to buy some souvenirs and traditional snacks..."
Soal 24
How did the writer feel about the trip at the end?
Kunci: D. Kalimat terakhir: "Although we were exhausted, everyone felt happy."
Soal 25
"The view from the top was breathtaking..." What is the closest meaning of the underlined word?
Kunci: A. 'Breathtaking' berarti sangat menakjubkan/mempesona.

Read the text below to answer questions 26 to 30!

The Battle of Surabaya
The Battle of Surabaya was fought between pro-independence Indonesian soldiers and British troops. On October 30, 1945, Brigadier General Mallaby was killed in Surabaya. This incident enraged the British forces.

Lieutenant General Sir Philip Christison issued an ultimatum demanding Indonesians to surrender their weapons by 6:00 a.m. on November 10, 1945. However, the Indonesian people refused to surrender.

Led by Bung Tomo, the young fighters fought bravely against the heavily armed British forces. Although thousands of Indonesians lost their lives, this battle became a national symbol of Indonesian resistance and is remembered as Heroes' Day (Hari Pahlawan).

Soal 26
What event caused the British forces to become enraged?
Kunci: B. "...Brigadier General Mallaby was killed... This incident enraged the British forces."
Soal 27
When was the deadline of the ultimatum given by the British force?
Kunci: C. "...surrender their weapons by 6:00 a.m. on November 10, 1945."
Soal 28
Who was the figure that inspired and led the Indonesian fighters during the battle?
Kunci: A. "Led by Bung Tomo, the young fighters fought bravely..."
Soal 29
What is the text purpose?
Kunci: D. Teks bertujuan menceritakan kembali peristiwa sejarah Pertempuran Surabaya.
Soal 30
"However, the Indonesian people refused to surrender." What is the synonym of "refused"?
Kunci: B. Refused artinya menolak, sinonimnya adalah Rejected.
